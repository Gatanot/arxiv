# AI assistance and responsibility

OpenAI Codex contributed substantially to construction discovery,
combinatorial arguments, proof simplification, Lean formalization,
computational checking, public literature searches, and manuscript drafting.
The work was directed by a human researcher. It was not language editing only.

The Lean kernel checks the released theorems under the axioms listed in the
verification report. Independent Python routines provide finite cross-checks.
These checks do not establish novelty, a third-party replication, or peer review.

The human author is responsible for the final content, references, attribution,
and submission. This record does not assert that an independent human expert
has reviewed every step. It does not infer exact model versions, cumulative
compute usage, or an immutable interaction history that is not available.
